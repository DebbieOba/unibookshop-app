## 1. Statutory & Regulatory Framework Assessed

### A. Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA 2023)
- **Status:** **APPLICABLE (Category A - Legally Compulsory)**
- **Role:** Data Controller (Bookstore owner) & Data Processor (Platform hosting / payment intermediaries).
- **Core Obligations:**
  - §24: Principles of processing (lawfulness, fairness, transparency, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity).
  - §25: Lawful basis (Contractual necessity for book orders; Legal obligation for financial receipts; Consent for marketing/push).
  - §32: Designation of Data Protection Officer (DPO) if classified as a Data Controller of Major Importance (DCMI) under NDPC GAID 2025.
  - §34-39: Data Subject Rights (Access, Rectification, Erasure/Deletion, Objection, Portability).
  - §40: Mandatory Security Measures & §41: Data Breach Notification within 72 hours to NDPC and affected data subjects.

### B. NDPC General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 (GAID 2025)
- **Status:** **APPLICABLE (Category A - Legally Compulsory)**
- **Audit Mandate:** Assessment whether the bookstore meets thresholds of processing personal data of > 1,000 data subjects within 6 months, requiring statutory annual audit filing via a licensed DPCO.

### C. Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 (FCCPA 2018)
- **Status:** **APPLICABLE (Category A - Legally Compulsory)**
- **Consumer Rights (§114 - §133):** Right to clear pricing in Naira (NGN), right to cancellation/refund for unfulfilled orders, right to fair contract terms, prohibition of deceptive representations regarding book stock condition (New vs Used).

### D. Cybercrimes (Prohibition, Prevention, etc.) Act 2015 (as amended)
- **Status:** **APPLICABLE (Category A - Legally Compulsory)**
- **Obligations:** System integrity, audit log retention, strict safeguards against unauthorized access to user accounts.

### E. CBN Regulatory Framework for Sandbox / Electronic Payments
- **Status:** **APPLICABLE (Category A - Legally Compulsory)**
- **Payment Processing:** Operations rely on CBN-licensed Payment Solution Service Providers (PSSP): Paystack Payments Ltd and Flutterwave Payment Solutions. Platform stores zero Primary Account Numbers (PAN), PINs, or CVVs.

## 2. Platform Compliance Mapping (Apple & Google)

### A. Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Category B - Platform Mandatory)
- **Guideline 5.1.1 (Data Collection & Privacy):** In-app Privacy Policy accessible without login; explicit user consent; Data Privacy Nutrition Label accuracy.
- **Guideline 5.1.1(v) (Account Deletion):** Apps supporting account creation must provide straightforward, in-app account deletion that purges personal identity records.
- **Guideline 3.1.5(a) (Physical Goods & Services Exemption):** Physical books delivered to customers are exempt from Apple In-App Purchase (IAP) 30% fee and legally utilize direct payment gateways (Paystack/Flutterwave).

### B. Google Play Developer Policies (Category B - Platform Mandatory)
- **User Data Policy:** Publicly accessible external account deletion web page matching in-app deletion.
- **Data Safety Section:** Complete disclosure of data collected (Name, Email, Phone, Address, Purchase History) and third-party sharing (Paystack, Flutterwave, Cloudinary, Resend).
- **Billing Policy:** Physical textbook delivery falls outside Google Play Billing requirement.
