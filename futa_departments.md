# Academic Departments at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA)

The academic departments currently functioning at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA) are organized across its specialized Schools (Faculties) and College.

---

## 1. School of Engineering and Engineering Technology (SEET)
*   Agricultural Engineering (AGE)
*   Biomedical Engineering (BME)
*   Chemical Engineering (CEE)
*   Civil Engineering (CVE)
*   Computer Engineering (CPE)
*   Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
*   Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE)
*   Information and Communication Engineering (ICE) / Information and Communications Technology
*   Mechanical Engineering (MEE)
*   Mechatronics Engineering (MCE)
*   Metallurgical and Materials Engineering (MME)
*   Mining Engineering (MNE)

## 2. School of Computing (SOC)
*   Computer Science (CSC)
*   Cyber Security (CYS)
*   Information Systems (IFS)
*   Information Technology (IFT)
*   Software Engineering (SEN)

## 3. School of Agriculture and Agricultural Technology (SAAT)
*   Agricultural Extension and Communication Technology (AEC)
*   Agricultural and Resource Economics (ARE)
*   Animal Production and Health (APH)
*   Crop, Soil and Pest Management (CSP)
*   Ecotourism and Wildlife Management (EWM)
*   Fisheries and Aquaculture Technology (FAT)
*   Food Science and Technology (FST)
*   Forestry and Wood Technology (FWT)
*   Nutrition and Dietetics (NTD)

## 4. School of Environmental Technology (SET)
*   Architecture (ARC)
*   Building Technology (BDG)
*   Estate Management (ESM)
*   Industrial Design (IDD)
*   Quantity Surveying (QSV)
*   Surveying and Geoinformatics (SVG)
*   Urban and Regional Planning (URP)

## 5. College of Health Sciences
### School of Basic Medical Sciences (SBMS)
*   Human Anatomy (ANA)
*   Biomedical Technology (BIM)
*   Medical Laboratory Science (MLS)
*   Medicine and Surgery (MBBS)
*   Physiology (PHS)

## 6. School of Physical Sciences (SPS)
*   Chemistry (CHM)
*   Mathematical Sciences (MTS)
*   Physics (PHY) *(with options in Electronics and Energy)*
*   Statistics (STA)

## 7. School of Life Sciences (SLS)
*   Biochemistry (BCH)
*   Biology (BIO)
*   Biotechnology (BTH)
*   Microbiology (MCB)

## 8. School of Earth and Mineral Sciences (SEMS)
*   Applied Geology (AGY)
*   Applied Geophysics (AGP)
*   Marine Science and Technology (MST)
*   Meteorology and Climate Science (MCS)
*   Remote Sensing and Geoinformatics (RSG)

## 9. School of Logistics and Innovation Technology (SLIT)
*   Business Information Technology (BIT)
*   Business Management Technology (BMT)
*   Entrepreneurship Management Technology (ENT)
*   Logistics and Transport Technology (LTT)
*   Project Management Technology (PMT)
*   Security and Investment Management Technology (SIMT)
