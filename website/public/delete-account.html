Google Play & NDPA Compliance

Delete Your BOOKDECK Account

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA §37) and Google Play User Data Policies, you may submit a request to permanently delete your BOOKDECK account and associated personal data.

What will be deleted immediately:
  • Your profile details (Full name, phone number, profile photo)
  • Saved delivery addresses and contact preferences
  • Push notification tokens, active sessions, and credentials
Retention notice: Pursuant to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and financial audit regulations, financial purchase and payment receipts are retained in an unlinked, anonymized form for 7 years.
You can also delete your account directly inside the BOOKDECK mobile app under Account → Security → Delete Account.
